About

I’m currently pursuing an M.Sc. in Economics with a specialization in Data Analytics at Symbiosis School of Economics, Pune. My interest lies in combining the way economics helps us understand problems with the way data can help us analyse and solve them.

My academic background has given me a foundation in economics, econometrics, statistics, and data mining, while my specialization has helped me build practical skills in Python, SQL, R, Power BI, and Excel. I’ve also been exploring Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning and how they can be applied to analytical problems.

Through my projects, I’ve worked on SQL-based sales analysis, Power BI dashboards, and crude oil price forecasting using ARDL and ARIMA. I enjoy working with data, finding patterns, and understanding what those patterns actually mean. I’m particularly interested in building a career at the intersection of economics, data, and technology.

Econometric & Explainable Machine Learning Analysis

My ongoing dissertation research examines whether firm-level characteristics can help predict future export intensity of Indian listed manufacturing firms. The study combines econometric analysis with machine-learning models to distinguish explanatory relationships from out-of-sample predictive performance, with SHAP-based explainability used to interpret model predictions.
